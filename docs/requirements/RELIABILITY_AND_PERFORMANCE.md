# Reliability, Performance, and Failure-Handling Requirements

**Scope:** Failure visibility, resource bounds, concurrency, fallbacks, timeouts, resilience, stale state, performance optimization, and integrity-preserving degradation.  
**Parent requirements:** [Global requirements](GLOBAL_REQUIREMENTS.md)

## Reliability requirements

| ID              | Requirement                                                                                                                                                                             | Acceptance criteria                                                                                                                                                                       | Parent                          | Implementation trace                                        | Verification                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **PRD-NFR-101** | Failures that can affect segmentation, metadata, attribution, citation, evidence, publication, persistence, or durable operation state MUST be surfaced rather than silently swallowed. | Injected dependency/storage failures result in explicit errors/build warnings/job failures and do not masquerade as valid scholarly absence or success.                                   | PRD-G-025                       | [AGENTS](../../AGENTS.md)                                   | **Direct:** [failure visibility](../../tests/test_failure_visibility.py)                                                                                                                                                        |
| **PRD-NFR-102** | Operational failure MUST remain distinguishable from valid no-result/no-supported-value conditions.                                                                                     | Provider outage, parser exception, vector failure, timeout, and malformed output are not converted into a FieldAssertion confirmed absence or ordinary zero-result state.                 | PRD-G-004, PRD-G-025            | [cELF](../../SPECIFICATION.md)                              | **Direct/Partial:** [FieldAssertions](../../tests/test_field_assertions.py); [progressive metadata retrieval](../../tests/test_progressive_metadata_retrieval.py); [failure visibility](../../tests/test_failure_visibility.py) |
| **PRD-NFR-103** | Resource-intensive ingestion MUST enforce byte/decompression/image/audio/tool bounds before or during processing.                                                                       | Malformed/oversized/resource-bomb inputs cannot create unbounded memory/CPU work before validation.                                                                                       | PRD-G-033                       | [Ingestion validation](../INGESTION_VALIDATION.md)          | **Direct:** [ingestion boundaries](../../tests/test_ingestion_boundaries.py)                                                                                                                                                    |
| **PRD-NFR-104** | Large-corpus processing MUST be bounded and resumable where feasible.                                                                                                                   | Segmentation/enrichment/upsert/capture/retrieval use batching, checkpoints, candidate limits, or equivalent mechanisms rather than unbounded whole-corpus materialization when avoidable. | PRD-G-024, PRD-G-033            | [Architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md)                          | **Direct/Partial:** [corpus build resilience](../../tests/test_corpus_build_resilience.py); [packet reduction](../../tests/test_packet_reduction.py); capture/upsert tests                                                      |
| **PRD-NFR-105** | Concurrency MUST not allow automatic work to overwrite concurrent human review decisions.                                                                                               | Human edits made while enrichment runs remain authoritative and later worker merges respect current canonical authority.                                                                  | PRD-G-005, PRD-G-033            | review/enrichment contract                                  | **Direct:** [human metadata ownership](../../tests/test_human_metadata_ownership.py); [enrichment cycles](../../tests/test_enrichment_cycles.py); [review queues](../../tests/test_review_queues.py)                            |
| **PRD-NFR-106** | Fallback behavior MUST be explicit, bounded, and traceable.                                                                                                                             | A timeout/unavailable/error fallback records reason/type and does not silently switch semantics or bypass required validators.                                                            | PRD-G-010, PRD-G-025, PRD-G-033 | pipeline/retrieval contracts                                | **Direct:** [progressive metadata retrieval](../../tests/test_progressive_metadata_retrieval.py); [evidence pipeline](../../tests/test_evidence_pipeline.py); [pipeline tracing](../../tests/test_pipeline_tracing.py)          |
| **PRD-NFR-107** | Failure of a derived projection/index/cache MUST not corrupt or roll back an already-saved canonical decision unless the canonical transaction itself failed.                           | Review/save success remains true even when metadata-memory/vector side effects fail; failure is reported separately.                                                                      | PRD-G-003, PRD-G-025            | memory/review contract                                      | **Direct:** [metadata batch decisions](../../tests/test_metadata_batch_decisions.py); [failure visibility](../../tests/test_failure_visibility.py)                                                                              |
| **PRD-NFR-108** | Restart recovery MUST prefer explicit interruption/failure over silent replay of non-idempotent background work.                                                                        | Jobs interrupted by process restart are marked failed/interrupted; users may deliberately retry.                                                                                          | PRD-G-032                       | job persistence contract                                    | **Direct:** [SQLite persistence](../../tests/test_sqlite_persistence.py)                                                                                                                                                        |
| **PRD-NFR-109** | Realtime notification failure MUST not determine durable operation success.                                                                                                             | Jobs remain correct/queryable if WebSocket publication fails; clients can resynchronize.                                                                                                  | PRD-G-003, PRD-G-033            | [Realtime](../REALTIME.md)                                  | **Direct:** [realtime plane](../../tests/test_realtime_plane.py)                                                                                                                                                                |
| **PRD-NFR-110** | Slow realtime clients MUST remain bounded.                                                                                                                                              | Outgoing queue/coalescing/replay windows impose finite memory/event bounds.                                                                                                               | PRD-G-033                       | [Realtime](../REALTIME.md)                                  | **Direct:** [realtime plane](../../tests/test_realtime_plane.py); [realtime live hints](../../tests/test_realtime_live_hints.py)                                                                                                |
| **PRD-NFR-111** | Large semantic/evidence/context packets SHOULD be reduced through deterministic bounded selection rather than arbitrary truncation that destroys provenance.                            | Reduction preserves identity/selection rationale needed to audit what reached the model/UI.                                                                                               | PRD-G-002, PRD-G-033            | pipeline/context architecture                               | **Direct/Partial:** [packet reduction](../../tests/test_packet_reduction.py); [pipeline benchmark](../../tests/test_pipeline_benchmark.py)                                                                                      |
| **PRD-NFR-112** | Performance metrics MUST be interpreted as operational measurements rather than scholarly quality guarantees.                                                                           | Latency/p95/fallback frequency/candidate counts do not become claims about evidence validity or answer truth.                                                                             | PRD-G-034                       | [PR #280](https://github.com/ajschlosser/DerridAI/pull/280) | **Direct/Partial:** [System Data pipelines](../../web/tests/frontend/system-data-pipelines.test.ts)                                                                                                                             |
