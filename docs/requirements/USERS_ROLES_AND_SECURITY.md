# Users, Roles, Capabilities, and Security Requirements

**Scope:** Authentication, users, roles, capability enforcement, researcher/admin boundaries, privacy filtering, secrets, destructive administration, and realtime authorization.  
**Parent requirements:** [Global requirements](GLOBAL_REQUIREMENTS.md)

## Authentication and session requirements

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance criteria | Parent | Implementation trace | Verification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **PRD-USR-001** | Protected application/API functionality MUST require an authenticated session unless a route is explicitly public. | Missing/invalid sessions receive an authentication failure and do not obtain protected data through REST, GraphQL, or realtime transports. | PRD-G-018 | [Architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md) | **Direct:** [auth hardening](../../tests/test_auth_hardening.py); [GraphQL façade](../../tests/test_graphql_facade.py); [realtime plane](../../tests/test_realtime_plane.py) |
| **PRD-USR-002** | Failed-login handling MUST resist repeated password guessing without disclosing whether a username exists. | Login throttling/lockout applies after configured failures and responses do not become a username-enumeration oracle. | PRD-G-018 | authentication contract | **Direct:** [auth hardening](../../tests/test_auth_hardening.py) |
| **PRD-USR-003** | Session-cookie security attributes MUST be configurable for secure deployment. | HTTPS deployments can enable the Secure cookie flag through configuration; invalid setting values do not silently produce unsafe behavior. | PRD-G-018 | deployment/auth contract | **Direct:** [auth hardening](../../tests/test_auth_hardening.py) |
| **PRD-USR-004** | Logout/account reset/destructive reset operations MUST invalidate affected sessions as required by their scope. | A full system reset expires the current session and returns the system to bootstrap-required state. | PRD-G-018, PRD-NFR-002 | administrative reset contract | **Direct:** [fresh-install/NUKE](../../tests/test_nuke_fresh_install.py) |

## Role and capability requirements

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance criteria | Parent | Implementation trace | Verification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **PRD-USR-005** | Authorization MUST be capability-based at the server boundary rather than relying only on navigation visibility or coarse client-side role checks. | Restricted REST/GraphQL/realtime operations reject callers without the required capability even if they construct requests manually. | PRD-G-018 | [Architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md); [GraphQL](../GRAPHQL.md); [Realtime](../REALTIME.md) | **Direct:** [roles and RAG validation](../../tests/test_roles_and_rag_request_validation.py); [GraphQL façade](../../tests/test_graphql_facade.py); [realtime plane](../../tests/test_realtime_plane.py) |
| **PRD-USR-006** | Administrator role semantics MUST remain explicit and protected from accidental privilege reduction through ordinary role editing. | Built-in administrator is represented as locked full access and cannot be edited like a normal custom role. | PRD-G-018, PRD-G-031 | roles contract | **Direct:** [Roles view](../../web/tests/frontend/roles-view.test.ts); [roles domain](../../web/tests/frontend/roles-domain.test.ts) |
| **PRD-USR-007** | The product SHOULD support custom roles composed from the central capability catalog. | Custom role IDs/permissions validate against known capabilities; deleted roles cannot leave users with invalid role references. | PRD-G-018, PRD-G-035 | roles contract | **Direct:** [roles and RAG validation](../../tests/test_roles_and_rag_request_validation.py); [Roles view](../../web/tests/frontend/roles-view.test.ts) |
| **PRD-USR-008** | Capability names/categories displayed in the UI MUST be localizable. | Every capability/category in the catalog has matching supported-locale copy rather than exposing raw IDs. | PRD-G-027 | roles/i18n contract | **Direct:** [roles and RAG validation](../../tests/test_roles_and_rag_request_validation.py) |
| **PRD-USR-009** | Researcher-visible navigation SHOULD reflect capabilities but MUST NOT be treated as the enforcement boundary. | Inaccessible pages/actions are omitted/disabled appropriately, while server authorization independently rejects unauthorized requests. | PRD-G-018, PRD-G-019 | shell/security contract | **Partial:** [research workspace](../../web/tests/frontend/research-workspace.test.ts); [researcher route policy](../../tests/test_researcher_route_policy.py) |

## Privacy and information-boundary requirements

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance criteria | Parent | Implementation trace | Verification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **PRD-USR-010** | Researcher responses MUST omit administrator-only infrastructure/configuration detail while preserving information needed to use researcher workflows. | Health/config responses expose workspace availability/readiness but not internal configuration or protected diagnostics. | PRD-G-018, PRD-G-031 | researcher route policy | **Direct:** [researcher route policy](../../tests/test_researcher_route_policy.py) |
| **PRD-USR-011** | Plaintext content-policy terms MUST remain administrator-only when the researcher contract permits only a hashed mirror. | Researchers can obtain the hashed policy required for local filtering but cannot read/write protected plaintext term lists. | PRD-G-018 | content-policy route contract | **Direct:** [researcher route policy](../../tests/test_researcher_route_policy.py) |
| **PRD-USR-012** | Blind/second-opinion review state MUST be scrubbed from every response channel visible to the reviewer who must not see the prior answer. | REST/GraphQL/derived graph responses that can carry Record-derived values are filtered; new relevant routes must be classified so they cannot accidentally leak sealed values. | PRD-G-005, PRD-G-018 | response-filter contract | **Direct:** [second-opinion scrub](../../tests/test_second_opinion_scrub.py); [GraphQL façade](../../tests/test_graphql_facade.py) |
| **PRD-USR-013** | Owner-scoped claim/response memory MUST not cross user boundaries. | Users cannot validate/use another owner's private claim/response memory unless an explicit capability/product rule permits it. | PRD-G-016, PRD-G-018 | memory/security contract | **Direct:** [claim memory](../../tests/test_claim_memory.py); [unified memory/provenance](../../tests/test_unified_memory_provenance.py) |
| **PRD-USR-014** | Realtime subscriptions/events MUST enforce topic and audience authorization independently of the WebSocket connection being authenticated. | A connected user cannot subscribe to unauthorized topics or receive another user's job events; role with no allowed realtime topic is refused. | PRD-G-018 | [Realtime](../REALTIME.md) | **Direct:** [realtime plane](../../tests/test_realtime_plane.py); [realtime live hints](../../tests/test_realtime_live_hints.py) |

## Secret and destructive-operation requirements

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance criteria | Parent | Implementation trace | Verification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **PRD-USR-015** | API keys, tokens, passwords, and equivalent secrets MUST NOT be emitted into public config, scholarly provenance, logs/traces intended for ordinary inspection, or shareable URL state. | Secret-bearing inputs are write-only/redacted at public serialization boundaries; build/provider history records identity without secret material. | PRD-G-018, PRD-NFR-003 | provider/connection/security contracts | **Direct/Partial:** [editorial memory/provider switch](../../tests/test_editorial_memory_and_provider_switch.py); [Chroma connection](../../tests/test_chroma_connection.py); route-state tests |
| **PRD-USR-016** | Backup creation MUST warn when the archive will contain stored provider credentials. | UI can determine whether configured profiles contribute credentials and confirmation copy reflects the sensitivity of the archive. | PRD-G-018, PRD-G-023 | backup security contract | **Direct:** [backup workspace](../../web/tests/frontend/backup-workspace.test.ts) |
| **PRD-USR-017** | System-wide destructive reset MUST be administrator-only, explicit, and restore a known bootstrap state. | Reset removes intended users/custom roles/corpus/system state, preserves built-in role definitions as specified, and requires bootstrap again. | PRD-G-018, PRD-G-032, PRD-NFR-002 | administrative reset contract | **Direct:** [fresh-install/NUKE](../../tests/test_nuke_fresh_install.py) |
