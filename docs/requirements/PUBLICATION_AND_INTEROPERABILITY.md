# Publication, Export, Import, and Interoperability Requirements

**Scope:** Corpus publication, validation, public JSONL, citations, integrity, import/export, cELF interoperability, and standards mappings.  
**Parent requirements:** [Global requirements](GLOBAL_REQUIREMENTS.md)

## Publication requirements

| ID              | Requirement                                                                                                                                                                              | Acceptance criteria                                                                                                                                                                                 | Parent                          | Implementation trace                                                                                                     | Verification                                                                                                                                                                           |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **PRD-PUB-001** | Publication MUST operate on reviewed/eligible Records and MUST NOT silently publish unresolved required metadata.                                                                        | Publication is blocked when required validation gates fail; accepted/rejected/unresolved state is handled explicitly.                                                                               | PRD-G-011, PRD-G-017            | [cELF](../../SPECIFICATION.md); [User Guide](../USER_GUIDE.md)                                                           | **Direct:** [publication lifecycle](../../tests/test_publication_lifecycle.py)                                                                                                         |
| **PRD-PUB-002** | Rejected Records MUST be excluded from required-metadata/publication checks for the publishable corpus while an all-rejected build becomes an explicit no-publishable-records state.     | Mixed builds can publish eligible accepted Records; all-rejected builds do not create a misleading empty successful corpus.                                                                         | PRD-G-017, PRD-G-025            | publication lifecycle contract                                                                                           | **Direct/Partial:** [publication lifecycle](../../tests/test_publication_lifecycle.py); [verse cleanup/rejected Records](../../tests/test_verse_cleanup_and_rejected_records.py)       |
| **PRD-PUB-003** | Public JSONL MUST materialize scholarly values that consumers need even when canonical internal authority is represented through FieldAssertions.                                        | A consumer that does not implement FieldAssertion internals can still read the selected scholarly value; provenance/audit artifacts remain available through cELF/related exports where applicable. | PRD-G-004, PRD-G-017            | [FieldAssertion migration](../FIELD_ASSERTION_MIGRATION.md); [PR #274](https://github.com/ajschlosser/DerridAI/pull/274) | **Direct:** [DerridAI ledger](../../tests/test_derridai_ledger.py); [publication lifecycle](../../tests/test_publication_lifecycle.py)                                                 |
| **PRD-PUB-004** | Document/work-level metadata inherited by Records MUST survive publication according to the schema/materialization contract.                                                             | Title/author/etc. resolved through canonical assertions are not stripped from published rows.                                                                                                       | PRD-G-017                       | publication contract                                                                                                     | **Direct:** [publication lifecycle](../../tests/test_publication_lifecycle.py) (`test_publication_keeps_document_level_manifest_metadata`)                                             |
| **PRD-PUB-005** | Publication artifacts MUST expose integrity data sufficient to detect accidental modification.                                                                                           | Archive/content cryptographic hashes are generated and an integrity file/manifest refers to the published artifact.                                                                                 | PRD-G-014, PRD-G-017            | publication contract                                                                                                     | **Direct:** [publication lifecycle](../../tests/test_publication_lifecycle.py)                                                                                                         |
| **PRD-PUB-006** | Build/Record warnings that materially affect interpretation SHOULD travel with publication rather than disappearing at export.                                                           | Provenance-relevant warnings and acknowledgements remain associated with the appropriate Record/publication.                                                                                        | PRD-G-002, PRD-G-017            | publication contract                                                                                                     | **Direct:** [publication lifecycle](../../tests/test_publication_lifecycle.py)                                                                                                         |
| **PRD-PUB-007** | Publication MUST preserve stable Record identity and appropriate revision/source references.                                                                                             | Public Record identity is not replaced by export row number, browser ID, or vector ID; source/citation relationships remain reconstructible at the supported conformance level.                     | PRD-G-002, PRD-G-009, PRD-G-017 | [cELF](../../SPECIFICATION.md)                                                                                           | **Direct/Partial:** [publication lifecycle](../../tests/test_publication_lifecycle.py); [research object graph](../../tests/test_research_object_graph.py)                             |
| **PRD-PUB-008** | Publication serialization MUST exclude internal transient review/runtime state that is not part of the public corpus contract without deleting the underlying authoritative/audit state. | Temporary queue/UI/job fields do not leak into public JSONL merely because they exist in a build Record.                                                                                            | PRD-G-003, PRD-G-017            | publication schema contract                                                                                              | **Direct/Partial:** [source quality/publication schema](../../tests/test_source_quality_and_publication_schema.py); [publication lifecycle](../../tests/test_publication_lifecycle.py) |

## Citation and interchange requirements

| ID              | Requirement                                                                                                                                               | Acceptance criteria                                                                                                                                                  | Parent                          | Implementation trace                                                                     | Verification                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **PRD-PUB-009** | Exported/rendered citations MUST preserve the best supported source locator without conflating physical PDF position and scholarly printed page identity. | Printed labels, PDF pages, audio time ranges, and other media locators retain their distinct meanings.                                                               | PRD-G-008, PRD-NFR-011          | [cELF Citation](../../SPECIFICATION.md)                                                  | **Direct/Partial:** [semantic atoms/MLA citations](../../tests/test_semantic_atoms_and_mla_citations.py); [document layout](../../tests/test_document_layout.py); [research object graph](../../tests/test_research_object_graph.py) |
| **PRD-PUB-010** | Import/export round trips MUST preserve stable scholarly identity and supported metadata semantics, not just display text.                                | Reimport of supported DerridAI/cELF-compatible data preserves IDs/field identities/revisions where the format carries them; lossy formats declare their limitations. | PRD-G-009, PRD-G-017, PRD-G-039 | [cELF](../../SPECIFICATION.md)                                                           | **Partial/Gap:** publication/import tests cover current JSONL paths; a dedicated full cELF round-trip conformance suite should expand this requirement                                                                               |
| **PRD-PUB-011** | cELF interoperability mappings MUST preserve cELF semantic distinctions rather than merely renaming fields into another standard.                         | Mapping Record/RecordRevision, assertions, evidence, runs, claims, and support bindings retains identity/provenance distinctions or records an explicit loss.        | PRD-G-002, PRD-G-014            | [cELF Interoperability Architecture](../../SPECIFICATION.md)                             | **Architectural/Gap:** specification-defined; automated cross-standard conformance coverage should be added as exporters mature                                                                                                      |
| **PRD-PUB-012** | PROV export SHOULD preserve persistent Record identity and revision relationships.                                                                        | Successive RecordRevisions remain distinguishable and related to the persistent Record/prior revisions through PROV-compatible relations/extensions.                 | PRD-G-009, PRD-G-014            | [cELF PROV Mapping Profile](../../SPECIFICATION.md)                                      | **Gap:** no dedicated PROV export conformance test identified                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **PRD-PUB-013** | RO-Crate interoperability MAY compose with suitable generic profiles but MUST retain research-specific cELF semantics needed for traceability.            | Crate export does not flatten generated claims/support/evidence/provenance into opaque attachments.                                                                  | PRD-G-013, PRD-G-014            | [cELF RO-Crate Profile](../../SPECIFICATION.md)                                          | **Gap:** no dedicated RO-Crate conformance test identified                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **PRD-PUB-014** | Claim-level publication MAY support nanopublication-compatible packaging without weakening claim/support/provenance semantics.                            | Assertion/provenance/publication-info mappings preserve the cELF GeneratedClaim and SupportBinding relationships or document unsupported distinctions.               | PRD-G-013, PRD-G-017            | [cELF Claim-Level Publication and Nanopublication Compatibility](../../SPECIFICATION.md) | **Gap:** no dedicated nanopublication conformance test identified                                                                                                                                                                    |
