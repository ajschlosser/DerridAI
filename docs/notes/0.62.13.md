# 0.62.13 — Fair Falcon

Measuring the reviewers, not just the models. Built on 0.62.12.

- **Reviewer self-consistency:** with `experiment.recheck_rate` set, a random share of a reviewer's decisions is reopened blind after eight more decisions in the same build. The earlier answer is sealed in the ledger and scrubbed from the record; the second answer is scored against it, shown, and kept. Reported with a 95% interval.
- **Reviewer identity:** human decisions in the ledger carry a pseudonymous reviewer id (`user-<number>`, never a name). Background work carries none.
- **Blind second opinions:** with `experiment.iaa_rate` set, a random share of decisions is also labelled by a different reviewer who cannot see the first answer. The field appears blind in the ordinary editor; saving it records the second opinion and leaves the stored answer alone, then reveals the first. Agreement, Cohen's kappa and n are reported. A different reviewer answering a re-check counts as agreement between people, not self-consistency. `GET /api/pdf/corpus-builds/{id}/second-opinions` lists what is waiting.
- **Blind values scrubbed everywhere on the record:** the model's stored answer, its reasoning and its confidence aggregates, which the blind review shipped in 0.62.12 left in place.
- **Checked in the real app:** the measurements panel and the blind and re-check fields render against the mock API, and the panel has no axe violations.

## What is not done

- Second-opinion blinding covers the record list and metadata saves; accept and disposition responses may still carry the first answer. No queue view for pending second opinions. Corpus Builder is admin-only, so second reviewers must be administrators.
- Tokens and money (providers report no usage), power planning, exact model hashes, held-out-corpus generalization; bootstrap and McNemar are not used in any figure.
- The eight-decision re-check spacing is a fixed constant. No live-model run.
