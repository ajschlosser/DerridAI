# 0.62.8 — Spacious Stork

The Corpus Builder review workspace now fills the screen instead of starting below the fold, its decisions are one row you can always reach, and its panes resize from the keyboard.

- **A frame that fills the screen.** The queue, the record and the details sit in one frame under the top bar, and each scrolls on its own. On a wide screen there are three columns with two dividers; on a laptop the details sit under the queue and record; on a phone it is an ordinary page. Opening a build scrolls the workspace into place. Before, in a 900-pixel-tall window, the record grid began 160 pixels below the fold, under five stacked panels.
- **A page bug that made the page 4,700 pixels too tall.** The queue's visually-hidden labels sat in panes that were not positioned, so they escaped the pane's clipping and stretched the whole page, which is where the dead space below the workspace came from. Each pane is now its own positioned scroll box, and the page is 1,765 pixels tall where it was 6,550.
- **Decisions in one row.** The bar under the record is **More actions**, **Skip**, **Reject & next** and **Accept & next**, pinned to the bottom of the record pane; it was two rows and 102 pixels tall. **Combine with previous or next record**, **Slice record** and **Preview JSONL** are in the More actions menu, and an action that is not available stays in the list and says why (for example, that there is no previous record). **Undo** and **Redo** moved to the top of the record. In a narrow pane the menu button shrinks to its dots so the row still fits, down to a 1366 by 650 window.
- **A tidier toolbar.** Two rows instead of four: the queue tabs, then search, the mode buttons (shown as Record, Metadata and Source, with their full names kept for assistive technology) and **Accept clean**. **Bulk edit metadata** and **Reject selected** moved into a **Bulk actions** menu that says why Reject selected is unavailable until you select something.
- **Queue rows.** Each row shows a state icon (a shape as well as a colour) and the state once; it used to say "Metadata" twice.
- **Resizable panes.** Drag a divider, or focus it and use the arrow keys (Shift for larger steps, Home and End for the limits); double-click resets it. The widths are remembered, and the directions follow the writing direction. This is the alternative to dragging that WCAG 2.5.7 asks for.
- **The review summary bar** no longer pins itself over the workspace.
- **Translated.** The new labels and reasons have English and French keys.

## What is not done

- The metadata cards in the details pane are unchanged; they are still tall and busy. That is the next piece.
- The Focus view has its own copy of these actions and is unchanged. It is full width, so its bar fits.
- The 1024 by 768 layout works but is tight: the record text is a few lines above the decision bar. A window shorter than about 34 rem falls back to an ordinary page.
- I checked the phone layout only from screenshots of a full-page capture, which does not render sticky elements faithfully, and not on a device. The decision bar is offset above the app's fixed bottom navigation there, which I did not see working.
- Right-to-left resizing is covered by a unit test, not by looking at a right-to-left locale in the app.
- Everything here was checked against a mock build of 60 made-up records, not a real build. Real builds have longer text and identifiers than the mock.
- With the menu collapsed to its dots, a sighted user has to find "More actions" by its icon; its name is there for assistive technology, and a tooltip is not yet added.

## Validation

Linux, Python 3.12, Node 22, on the branch after merging `master` at 0.62.7:

- pytest: 222 passed. `ruff` and `mypy` passed. Vitest: 234 passed, including 13 new tests for the menu and the splitter. `eslint`, `vue-tsc`, the production build and the Storybook build passed.
- Playwright with axe (WCAG 2.0 to 2.2, A and AA): 172 passed, 50 skipped by design. New: the workspace, its open menu, its metadata mode and the whole page are scanned in light and dark, and 8 layout tests check that the frame fills the screen under the top bar with no phantom scroll, that the decisions are one row and inside the window, that each pane scrolls on its own, the laptop and short-window layouts, that a row's state is not colour alone, the menu's reasons and Escape behaviour, and the dividers by keyboard.
- My first cleanup of the old queue-dot styles deleted three shared status-dot rules by accident; I caught it by comparing every rule against the original and restored them.
